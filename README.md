## 📊 Interactive PowerBI Dashboards

The insights generated from the programmatic pipeline are fully integrated into interactive **PowerBI** operational dashboards. These dashboards translate complex customer segments and forecasting models into actionable, executive-level business intelligence.

### Module 1: Customer Loyalty & Seller Ecosystem Dynamics
This strategic view tracks the health of both sides of the marketplace over time, balancing geographic user penetration with partner merchant performance metrics.

#### 🔑 Key Metrics & Strategic Frameworks Monitored:
* **Geographic Customer Density & Loyalty**: A regional choropleth mapping layer visualizing localized user footprints paired with relative **Retention (%)** tiers (High $>3\%$, Mid $2.5\%-3\%$, Low $<2.5\%$).
* **Monthly Revenue Mix (Acquisition vs. Retention)**: A stacked column chart tracking the longitudinal evolution of new user acquisition capital alongside repeat customer revenue retention streams.
* **Stable Seller Growth**: A baseline trend chart detailing long-term lifecycle velocity for high-tenure merchant cohorts ($3+$ Months Tenure).
* **Seller Performance (Sales Volume vs. Service Quality)**: A scatter quadrant layout charting aggregate **Total Orders** against **Average Seller Rating** to dynamically audit supply-side friction and quality thresholds.
* **Macro Operational KPIs**: Instant-read high-level health card modules reporting **Repeat Sales % (5.62%)** and overall transactional **Cancellation Rate % (0.45%)**.
